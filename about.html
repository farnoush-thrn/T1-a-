About This Project

COS30045 Data Visualisation

This website was created as part of the COS30045 Data Visualisation unit. The primary goal is to demonstrate the ability to process, explore, and visualise real-world datasets effectively.


The current dataset focuses on televisions sold in the Australian market, exploring the relationship between screen sizes, technologies, brands, and their respective energy consumption (mode power and star ratings).
